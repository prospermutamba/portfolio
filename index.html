Prosper Mutamba Joseph Founder & Creative Director

I'm Prosper Mutamba Joseph, founder of Ghost Design Lab — a creative studio built on the belief that great design is never an accident.

With a deep passion for visual communication, I specialize in building brands that resonate, interfaces that convert, and content that captivates. Every project I take on starts with a single question: what story does this brand need to tell?

Based in the DRC and working globally, I bring together strategic thinking and aesthetic precision to deliver design solutions that don't just look good — they work.
